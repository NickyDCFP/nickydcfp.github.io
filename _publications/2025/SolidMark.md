  This paper introduces SolidMark, a new evaluation method that provides per-image memorization scores to better detect when diffusion models have memorized specific training images. We use SolidMark to re-evaluate existing memorization mitigation techniques and demonstrate its ability to assess pixel-level memorization in generative models.
