abstract: >-
  We introduce ArchWorld, a benchmark of 3D architectural landmarks with high geographic coverage and rich metadata, and use it to analyze where current 3D foundation models fall short. We find that model performance varies by geographic region and introduce an intervention scheme that improves performance while reducing these geographic disparities.
